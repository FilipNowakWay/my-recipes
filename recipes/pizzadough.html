Pizza Dough

🍕 Pizza Dough Description

A classic, versatile pizza dough made from basic pantry staples. It features a crisp exterior and a soft, chewy interior, providing the perfect homemade base for your favorite sauce, cheese, and toppings.

  • Prep time: 20 mins
  • Rise time: 1 hour
  • Servings: 2 medium pizza crusts

🥣 Ingredients

  • 11⁄2
  • 1 packet (2 1⁄4)
  • 1 table spoon sugar
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 31⁄2 to 4 cups of all-purpose flour

👨‍🍳 Steps

  1. In a large bowl, combine the warm water, yeast, and sugar. Let it sit for 5–10 minutes until the mixture becomes frothy.
  2. Stir in the olive oil and salt.
  3. Gradually add the flour, one cup at a time, mixing until a dough forms and pulls away from the sides of the bowl.
  4. Turn the dough out onto a lightly floured surface and knead for about 5–7 minutes until smooth and elastic.
  5. Place the dough in a greased bowl, cover it with a damp towel, and let it rise in a warm place for 1 hour (or until doubled in size).
  6. Punch down the dough, divide it in half, shape into your desired pizza crusts, and add your toppings!
