🌮 Simple Beef Tacos

Description

A savory, customizable Mexican-style dish featuring seasoned ground beef loaded into warm shells with fresh toppings.

🛒 Ingredients

1 lb ground beef, 1 packet taco seasoning, 12 taco shells, 1 cup shredded lettuce, 1 cup diced tomatoes, 1 cup cheddar cheese, and 0.5 cup sour cream.

👨‍🍳 Steps

Brown the beef in a skillet, stir in the taco seasoning and water to simmer, warm the taco shells, and assemble with beef, lettuce, tomatoes, cheese, and sour cream.
