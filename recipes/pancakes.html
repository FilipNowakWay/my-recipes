🥞 Fluffy Homemade Pancakes

Description:

A golden, fluffy breakfast classic with a soft interior and crisp edges.

🛒 Ingredients

1.5 cups flour, 3.5 tsp baking powder, 1 tsp salt, 1 tbsp sugar, 1.25 cups milk, 1 egg, and 3 tbsp melted butter.

👨‍🍳 Steps:

Mix dry and wet ingredients together, pour batter onto a hot pan, cook until browned on both sides, and serve warm!
